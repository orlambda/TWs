TWs

This is a basic website that lists trigger warnings for certain games and other media.

Lists are not exhaustive and may contain errors as it is not possible for me to guarantee I have caught every possible TW. You may wish to do your own research if you need an exhaustive list for game or other piece of media.

If a game is not listed it may be added eventually but please do your own research. As I avoid spoilers for games it can be difficult to give a good list of trigger warnings without help from someone who has played the game and knows how to communicate without spoiling the game.

Please note that some trigger warnings may be spoilers.

I may make the site pretty in the future but it is not currently a priority.
