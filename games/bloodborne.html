  • Child abandonment & death
  • Body horror
  • Torture
  • Pregnancy from and giving birth to supernatural beings

  • Consumption of umbilical cords
  • Ghosts & undead
  • Various beasts and supernatural beings
  • Hanged dead
  • Animal death
  • Spiders, arachnophobia
  • Illness
  • Medical horror
  • Forced medical experimentation

  • Needles

  • Unstable reality
