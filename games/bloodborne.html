Bloodborne TWs

This is a list of trigger warnings for Bloodborne. It may contain errors and is non-exhaustive. Some of these may also be spoilers.

  • Horror, psychological horror, jumpscares, threat
  • Pregnancy from and giving birth to supernatural beings
  • Consumption of umbilical cords
  • Various beasts and supernatural beings
  • Spiders
  • Forced medical experimentation
  • Blood and gore
  • Needles
  • Holes / trypophobia
  • Body horror
  • Religious groups
  • A mechanic named 'Frenzy'
  • Transformation from human into beast
  • Nightmares and dream loops
