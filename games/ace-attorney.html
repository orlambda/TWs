Ace Attorney

General TWs

This is a brief list of a few TWs that apply to some or all of the Ace Attorney games. See the next section for more detailed lists by game.

  • Murder
  • Loss of a family member
  • Implied attraction to a minor from an adult character
  • Corruption
  • Police
  • Organised crime
  • Blackmail
  • Unfair legal processes
  • Implied PTSD
